A.1 Rating guide

Ratings help sequence attention. They should be considered together with context, exposure, confidence and the effort required to improve the observed condition.

RatingReference rangeInterpretation
Critical9.0–10.0Immediate ownership is required because credible impact could be severe or widespread.
High7.0–8.9Prioritised action is required; delay materially increases exposure or weakens assurance.
Medium4.0–6.9Plan corrective work and track it through the normal improvement cycle.
Low0.1–3.9Address as part of routine maintenance, learning or quality improvement.
Info0.0Context, good practice or an opportunity that does not represent direct risk.

EvidenceMaterial that allows a reader to understand how the observation was reached.
Recommended actionA proportionate change intended to improve the observed condition.
ValidationA later review that confirms whether an action changed the condition as intended.
