Assessment method

A repeatable sequence for moving from observable attack surface to verified impact without exceeding the authorised boundary.

01Map

Discover hosts, services, routes and application entry points.

 02Model

Identify trust boundaries, identities and likely attack paths.

 03Verify

Test weaknesses manually and retain reproducible evidence.
04Control

Demonstrate impact with the minimum safe level of exploitation.

 05Trace

Connect individual findings into reachable attack sequences.

 06Transfer

Prioritise fixes and define objective retest criteria.

Evidence standard

RequirementExpected record
ReproducibleRequests, commands or steps allow an authorised reviewer to repeat the observation.
ScopedEvidence identifies the affected target and remains inside the approved test boundary.
Impact-ledThe record explains what control failed and the consequence an attacker could reach.
ActionableRemediation includes a concrete control change and a verification condition.
Safety rule. Exploitation should stop once the agreed impact is demonstrated. Destructive actions, persistence and access to unrelated data require separate authorisation.
