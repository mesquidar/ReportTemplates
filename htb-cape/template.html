A. Severity Rating Definitions

Severity CVSS Range Description
Critical 9.0 - 10.0 Vulnerabilities that can be easily exploited and result in complete system compromise, significant data breach, or severe business impact.
High 7.0 - 8.9 Serious vulnerabilities that could result in significant damage or data exposure if exploited.
Medium 4.0 - 6.9 Vulnerabilities that have limited impact or require specific conditions to exploit.
Low 0.1 - 3.9 Minor vulnerabilities with minimal security impact.
Info 0.0 Informational findings that represent best practice improvements rather than security vulnerabilities.
